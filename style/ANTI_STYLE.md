# Anti-style and Negative Prompt Contract

Always discourage horror, dark dreamcore, eerie liminal spaces, grotesque anatomy, exposed organs, disgusting digestive imagery, body horror, illness fear, body-shaming, dramatic weight-loss imagery, sad or distressed food characters, excessive medical equipment, generic corporate vector art, overly realistic clinical anatomy, unreadable text, random letters, watermarks, logos, copyrighted characters, artist-name imitation, cluttered composition, muddy colors, neon colors, hypersexualized characters, unsafe child imagery, and symbolic scenery presented as literal scientific anatomy.

Do not use a living artist's name as a style reference. Do not add random Chinese characters as decoration. Do not use recognizable franchise characters or copied art.

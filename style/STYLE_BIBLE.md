# Style Bible: Cute Chinese Dreamcore Floating-Island World

## North star

Every image is a tiny, evidence-based nutrition world floating among soft clouds. It should feel collectible, comforting, and full of discoverable details: one hero ecosystem, small satellite islands, friendly residents, bridges, gardens, mushrooms, stars, and blank science signboards waiting for programmatic labels.

## Emotional target

Warmth, wonder, safety, curiosity, and gentle encouragement. Use dreamy atmosphere without isolation, menace, uncanny emptiness, or horror. Dreamcore here means a soft childlike imaginative logic: miniature worlds, floating terrain, moonlit clouds, and slightly magical scale.

## Composition grammar

1. Place one readable hero island as the educational anchor.
2. Use smaller food islands to create a clear left-to-right or top-to-bottom learning path.
3. Connect islands with cloud paths, bridges, curved trails, or floating stepping stones.
4. Reserve a clean title area and a small caption area before generating the image.
5. Concentrate detail around the educational path; leave breathing room elsewhere.

## Shape and rendering language

Prefer round, plush, clay-toy, paper-cut, and softly layered terrain. Use thick gentle contours, matte pastel surfaces, tiny gardens, rounded windows, and expressive but simple faces. Keep microbe characters as symbolic sprites—not literal anatomy. Use soft depth and layered foreground/midground/background without photorealism.

## Chinese-inspired motifs

Use original, restrained references to auspicious-cloud curves, moon-gate silhouettes, small tiled-roof forms, garden bridges, paper-cut-like cloud edges, and mountain-and-cloud composition logic. Avoid random Chinese characters, costume collage, excessive dragons, red-lantern repetition, or imperial-palace imagery.

## Recurring cast

- **Mushroom guide:** a tiny beige mushroom with a leaf satchel, pointing toward the teaching path.
- **Food residents:** round oats, bean, corn, sweet potato, fruit, and vegetable characters with distinct textures.
- **Microbe sprites:** tiny friendly abstract circles, loops, and soft antennae gathered in a garden village.
- **Blank science signboards:** small clean boards for post-generation labels, never random decorative writing.

## Scientific guardrail

The world is a visual metaphor. The hero island may symbolize a gut ecosystem, but it must never be rendered as exposed organs, disgusting digestive imagery, or literal microscopic anatomy. The image prompt must name the metaphorical status explicitly.

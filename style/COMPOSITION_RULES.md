# Composition Rules

- Reserve a title safe zone in the upper-left or upper third.
- Reserve a caption safe zone at the bottom with a light panel or quiet sky.
- Limit primary teaching messages to three.
- Make the hero island the largest object and give it visual contrast.
- Keep the reading path explicit: food sources → cloud path → hero ecosystem.
- Use foreground decorative objects to create depth, not to hide the message.
- Keep characters small but legible; do not turn the scene into a pile of stickers.
- Keep important meaning independent of color alone.
- Never ask the image model to render long titles, citations, or paragraphs.
